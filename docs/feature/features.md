# 🌟 Features in 30 Seconds

**Everything you need. Nothing you don't.**

### 🗣️ Chat (The Brain)
Ask your AI anything about your own life. *"What next task to do today?"*
- **[👉 Try Chat](/docs/feature/chat-by-ai)**

### 📝 Notes (The Memory)
Write anything. The AI automatically **tags**, **summarizes**, and **links** it for you.
- **[👉 Try Notes](/docs/feature/notes)**

### ✅ Tasks (The Action)
Plan projects on Kanban boards. Ask the AI to **break down big goals** into small steps.
- **[👉 Try Tasks](/docs/feature/task)**

### 🔐 Info Vault (The Safe)
Store passwords, contacts, and asset details securely. The AI uses this for *your* answers only.
- **[👉 Try Vault](/docs/feature/info-vault)**

---

### 🛠️ Why AI Notes?

*   **🔍 Semantic Search**: Find things by meaning ("healthy dinner"), not just keywords.
*   **🔌 API-First**: Connect your own LLMs (OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama).
*   **🔒 Self-Hosted**: Your data never leaves your server.
